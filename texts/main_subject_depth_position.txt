01. Foreground Subject: Position the main subject prominently in the nearest depth plane, physically close to the camera but out of focus, with the rest of the environment extending behind it.
02. Midground Subject: Position the main subject in the middle depth plane, with visible space between the camera and the subject and additional environmental depth extending behind it.
03. Background Subject: Position the main subject far from the camera, deep within the scene, surrounded by substantial foreground and middle-distance space, appearing smaller due to perspective.
